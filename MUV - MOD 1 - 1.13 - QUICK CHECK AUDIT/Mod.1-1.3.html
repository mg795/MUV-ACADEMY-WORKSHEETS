Mod.1-1.3
Movement Marketing: Extend Reach, Reduce Cost.

Quick Check: Is This Right For Us?

A structured 30-minute self-audit before you commit to building a movement.

Before you start:

This worksheet is designed for any type of organisation – charity, social enterprise, SME or purpose‑led business. Use 'organisation' throughout unless your shorthand feels clearer. Work through this with at least one other person who will push back on your thinking.

Important note:

The mechanics work identically whether you are a charity or a business. Wherever you see supporter/prospect, use whichever term fits your organisation: donor, customer, client, volunteer, or member.

Your Organisation in One Line

The Readiness Audit: Four Quadrants

Write brief honest answers in each quadrant. 'I think so' is not an answer. Aim for specific examples.

1) People & Relationships

Who is already with you? Who believes in what you do but has not been asked to take a bigger role yet? Name individuals if you can, not just "our supporters or prospects."

2) Stories You Can Tell

What has already happened because of your work? Not impact statistics: a real story with a real person and a real change. Could you tell it in two minutes without looking at a screen?

3) Partners & Connectors

Who already trusts you enough to introduce you to others? Name organisations, venues, media contacts or individuals who could amplify your work if asked in the right way.

4) Habits & Rituals

What do people already do repeatedly because of your organisation? Events, conversations, content, traditions? Movements build on existing habits, not invented ones.

Signal Strength Check

Rate each signal honestly from 1 (weak or absent) to 5 (clear and proven). Base your rating on evidence you can name, not gut feeling.

Belief
Do supporters/prospects repeat your values in their own words?
Rating: /5
Story
Can someone describe what you do without your brochure?
Rating: /5
Trust
Do partners open doors for you without being asked?
Rating: /5
Habit
Do people return or engage without prompting?
Rating: /5
Invitation
Do people bring others in because they want to?
Rating: /5
Total
Total: /25
18 to 25: Strong foundation. Ready to build. | 10 to 17: Partial readiness. Address your weakest signal first. | Under 10: Build the basics before the movement.

Verdict

Tick the verdict that best reflects your honest assessment after completing Sections 1 and 2.

Ready to Build

Three or more quadrants have specific named examples and your Signal Strength total is 18 or above. Proceed to Lesson 1.4.

Nearly There

Two quadrants are strong and total is 10 to 17. Identify your weakest signal and set a 30-day target before revisiting this check.

Not Yet

Fewer than two strong quadrants or total under 10. Revisit Module 1 Lesson 1.2 and build the foundation first. This is not failure: it is the most useful answer you can give yourself right now.
