Movement Marketing: Extend Reach, Reduce Cost

SCENARIO 1C: Family-Run Construction Firm

Module 1 Scenario | SME Example

Organisation Overview

Organisation type Local family-run construction firm (builder, renovator, or specialist contractor)
Team size Two-four people (owner-operator, site lead, part-time admin or comms)
Customers Long-standing local clients, referral-led and some commercial contracts
Funding Project revenue, word-of-mouth pipeline
What this module teaches: Start within. Find where energy already exists. Check authenticity before you scale.

This family-run construction firm scenario is a practical movement marketing strategy example, showing how a local builder can grow reputation and referrals through community-led marketing rather than paid advertising. For small trades businesses, authentic brand marketing starts with what is already true – the craftsmanship, the local trust and the long-standing relationships customers hold. This 30-day plan shows how to surface that energy, focus it into six clear strands and turn it into a repeatable growth system that works without a big budget.

The Belief

Trade businesses already have movement energy. Loyal repeat customers, word of mouth reputation and pride in craft. But they never think to articulate it. The audit surfaces what is already true. The fit check stops them from borrowing trendy purpose language that does not match a practical craft-led business.

30 Day Movement Marketing Plan

Week 1: Discover What Is Already True
Day 1 to 2: Run the Movement Potential Audit. Interview six past customers (by phone or WhatsApp voice note): 'Why did you choose us? What would you tell a neighbour?' Interview three team members: 'What are you most proud of?' Map people, stories, partners and habits.
Day 3 to 4: Run the Authenticity and Issue Fit Check. Test: 'We are the best builders in town.' Can you prove it? 'We care about sustainability.' Do your actual practices reflect that? 'We are a community business.' What does that mean specifically? Output: a clear, honest position that is true and provable.
Day 5 to 7: Define your six strands: Before-and-after project stories, customer testimonials, craft and process explainers, team pride, supplier spotlights, community invites. Write one sentence the whole team can repeat: 'Honest work done properly lasts, and we will prove it.'
Week 2: Show the Work
Day 8 to 10: Create a Project Stories library. Six of your best before-and-after transformations with short customer quotes. Phone photos (authentic, not styled), a three-sentence caption explaining what was done and why. Post one per week on Facebook or Instagram.
Day 11 to 14: Film 3 sixty second "How We Do It" videos. One showing brickwork technique. One showing how you protect a customer's home during work. One showing the team having a brew and planning the day. Caption: 'This is what our business looks like on a Tuesday'.
Week 3: Connect Craft to Community
Day 15 to 17: Host a free Ask a Builder evening At a local pub or community hall. 90 minutes, open to anyone planning a renovation. Bring samples, answer questions honestly (do you actually need an extension, or would a conversion work better?), and share three project stories. Limit 25 people.
Day 18 to 21: Capture 5 attendee reflections. Post: '25 people came to Ask a Builder last Thursday. Here is what they wanted to know.' Collect email addresses for follow-up.
Week 4: Amplify and Sustain
Day 22 to 25: Launch monthly Built Right email. One project story, one honest tip ('three things to check before you hire any builder'), one community invite.
Day 26 to 28: Partner with one aligned local business Kitchen showroom, architect, or estate agent for cross-referral.
Day 29 to 30: Review signals: Enquiries, quotes issued, website visits, and email sign-ups. Schedule the next Ask a Builder evening.

What Good Looks Like by Day 30

1
Completed audit revealing existing customer loyalty and craft pride you never articulated.
2
Completed fit check with honest positioning and no borrowed purpose
3
6 project stories and three videos creating proof-based content
4
One Ask a Builder event creates trust before the sales conversation
5
A shift from 'we do building work' to 'honest work done properly lasts – ask our customers'

Why This Saves You Money and Still Grows

Less waste, more reuse.

Project stories and How We Do It videos are evergreen. One story works for months.

Earned distribution.

Customers share before and after photos naturally. Partner referrals replace paid ads.

Proof beats polish.

Phone photos of real work outperform stock imagery for trust in trades.

Partner leverage.

One kitchen showroom or an architect cross-referencing doubles qualified leads at zero cost.

How This Gets You More Customers

Awareness becomes involvement.

Ask a Builder creates trust before anyone asks for a quote.

Trust builds faster.

Before and after proof plus customer quotes reduce "will they do a good job?" anxiety.

Repeat loop.

Monthly email plus quarterly events plus partner referrals equals a steady pipeline without chasing leads.

Tracking: Non-Negotiable

Baseline (Day 0): 3 leading indicators (event RSVPs, email signups, social shares) and 2 lagging indicators (quotes issued, jobs won). Review weekly for 15 minutes.

How a 30 Day Plan Becomes a Movement

A 30-day plan becomes a movement when honest work done properly becomes an identity customers signal – our builder. They refer you without being asked, reducing acquisition costs because reputation compounds.
