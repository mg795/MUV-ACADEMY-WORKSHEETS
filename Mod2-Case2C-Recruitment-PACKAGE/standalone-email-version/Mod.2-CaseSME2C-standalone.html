SCENARIO 2C: Local Recruitment and Staffing Agency

Module 2 Scenario | SME Example

Organisation Overview

Organisation type Independent recruitment agency placing candidates locally (admin, trades, hospitality or specialist roles)
Team size Two people (owner consultant and marketing and ops coordinator)
Revenue Placement fees and retained search
Funding Placement fees, retained search and repeat client revenue
What this module teaches: Story structure (problem, belief, vision, invitation). The supporter journey from stranger to advocate. Inclusive formats. Meet people where they are.

This local recruitment and staffing agency scenario is a practical movement marketing strategy example designed for small teams who need to grow trust, referrals and placements without relying only on job boards or paid reach. Like the national community history programme, it shows how shared stories and the right spaces can connect people who might not otherwise meet.

Community-led marketing applies powerfully in this context. Placed candidates, hiring businesses and local partners who already believe in the service are the most credible and cost-effective channels available.

Authentic brand marketing for recruitment agencies starts with one honest shift: stop sounding like a job board and start showing how the right match changes lives and businesses. This 30 day plan shows exactly how to build the story, map the journey and amplify it.

The Belief

The right job changes a life. The right hire changes a business.

Recruitment firms are story rich but rarely use it. Every placement is a 'problem, belief, transformation' narrative. The Movement Story template forces them to articulate why they exist beyond 'we fill jobs'. The Supporter Journey Map maps candidate, client and community pathways.

Three Things to Keep Top of Mind

1
Paired stories (candidate plus employer from the same placement) are gold. They prove your value from both sides.
2
'Jobs and Brews' mornings in a local cafe do more for your pipeline than any job board listing.
3
Your LinkedIn content should make local businesses and candidates think of you first. Consistency is the differentiator.

30 Day Movement Marketing Plan

Week 1: Build the Movement Story
Day 1 to 2: Complete the Movement Story. Problem: 'Local businesses struggle to find reliable people. Local candidates struggle to find decent opportunities. Both waste time on platforms that do not understand this market.' Belief: 'Great matches start with knowing the people, not just the CV.' Vision: 'A local jobs market where businesses find people they trust and candidates find roles where they grow.' Invitation: 'Tell us who you need or tell us what you are looking for and we will make the introduction that matters.'
Day 3 to 5: Collect 8 stories (with consent). Four from placed candidates ('how this role changed things for me') and four from employers ('what finding the right person meant for our business').
Day 6 to 7: Create the 'Right Match' Stories series. Four paired stories (candidate plus employer from the same placement told together). Format: LinkedIn carousel, short blog and email feature.
Week 2: Map the Journey
Day 8 to 10: Complete the Supporter Journey Map with three role based pathways. Candidate: discovery, registration, placement, retention, referral ('I recommended you to my friend'). Client: enquiry, first placement, repeat client, referral partner. Community: awareness, event attendance, advocate.
Day 11 to 14: Design and publish the first two 'Right Match' Stories on LinkedIn. Tag the candidate and employer (with permission). Each post ends with: 'Know someone who is looking? Know a business that is hiring?'
Week 3: Bridge Two Communities
Day 15 to 17: Host a 'Jobs and Brews' morning. 90 minutes in a cafe or community venue. Invite 10 candidates and 10 local business owners. Speed introduction format: 5 minute conversations and facilitated matching. Not formal interviews. Relaxed human connections.
Day 18 to 21: Capture reflections. Post: '20 people met at Jobs and Brews last Saturday. Three interviews booked before the coffee went cold.'
Week 4: Sustain
Day 22 to 25: Launch the monthly 'Right Match' email. One placement story, one market insight, one employer tip, one candidate tip and one invitation.
Day 26 to 28: Partner for cross referral. Partner with one local training provider or college for cross referral.
Day 29 to 30: Review signals. Registrations, placements, LinkedIn engagement and event attendance.

What Good Looks Like by Day 30

1
A Movement Story that positions you as community connector, not CV shuffler.
2
A role based Journey Map with three distinct pathways.
3
Four 'Right Match' Stories published with real candidate plus employer pairs.
4
One 'Jobs and Brews' event creating trust based connections.
5
A shift from 'we recruit' to 'we connect the right people to the right opportunities'.
Do not forget: Tagged candidates and employers share 'Right Match' Stories to their networks. Every story reaches people you could never reach with a job ad. That is earned distribution.

Why This Saves You Money and Still Grows

Less waste, more reuse
'Right Match' Stories and 'Jobs and Brews' become repeatable fixtures. No reinvention.
Partners distribute for you
Candidates, employers, colleges and local venues help carry your story into networks you already want to reach. Free and trusted.
Proof beats polish
Real candidate and employer stories outperform generic recruitment advertising.
Emotional truth converts
When people meet in person at 'Jobs and Brews', trust forms faster than through a listing alone.

How This Gets You More Clients, Candidates and Referrals

Awareness becomes involvement
'Jobs and Brews' creates an emotional connection. The monthly 'Right Match' email sustains it.
Trust builds faster
Hearing real stories, meeting face to face and seeing the quality of introductions removes hesitation.
Repeat loop
Monthly 'Right Match' email plus repeat 'Jobs and Brews' events plus shared candidate and employer stories equals compounding local awareness.

Tracking: Non-Negotiable

Baseline (Day 0): 3 leading indicators (LinkedIn engagement, event RSVPs, candidate registrations) and 2 lagging indicators (placements made, repeat client rate). Review weekly for 15 minutes.

How a 30 Day Plan Becomes a Movement

A movement forms when 'Right Match' becomes your identity in the local market. Businesses call you first. Candidates register without being asked. Both refer to others. That is compound growth through reputation, not ad spend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does movement marketing work for a recruitment agency?
A: It shifts the agency from sounding like a job board to becoming a trusted local connector, using stories, events and repeatable pathways to build reputation and referrals.
Q: What is community-led marketing in this context?
A: It means placed candidates, employers and local partners help carry the message rather than the agency broadcasting alone. Real stories make the service more believable and more shareable.
Q: Why do the three pathways matter?
A: Because candidates, clients and the wider community each enter the story differently. Mapping each pathway makes it easier to move people from awareness to action and referral.
