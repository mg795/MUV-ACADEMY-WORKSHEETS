Fit Check: Can We Prove It?

Verify that every claim in your movement story is authentic and evidence-based

Why this matters: Movements that grow by giving are built on trust. If a claim in your story is vague, overstated or cannot be backed up, it will eventually unravel your reputation. This worksheet helps you audit authenticity and credibility before you scale. It applies to any type of organisation: charity, social enterprise, SME or purpose-led business. Work through it with someone willing to ask difficult questions. Your answers here feed directly into your Movement Story in Module 2.

The mechanics work identically whether you are a charity or a business. Wherever you see supporter/prospect, use whichever term fits your organisation: donor, customer, client, volunteer or member.

1. Claims Audit: What Are You Saying?

List up to four claims your organisation currently makes or plans to make. For each claim, choose a verdict and note the evidence that supports it. Be specific: 'we help people' is not a claim, it is a category.

2. Ethics and Credibility Check

Answer each question honestly. A 'No' is not a failure: it is a gap to address before you build.
Consent

Do the people in your stories know they are being used and have agreed?

Accuracy

Are your impact numbers independently verified or clearly estimated?

Recency

Is all evidence from the last 18 months? Nothing out of date presented as current?

Balance

Does your story acknowledge what has not yet worked as well as what has?

Specificity

Can you name real people, real places and real outcomes rather than general categories?

Repeatability

Would a supporter/prospect be comfortable repeating this claim to someone they know?

3. Authenticity Rating

Based on your Claims Audit and Ethics Check above, give your overall authenticity an honest rating. Tick one.
Green: Scale It

All claims are provable, evidence is recent and people have given consent. Your story is ready to travel. Move on to building your Movement Story in Module 2.

Amber: Build Proof First

One or two claims are thin. Evidence gaps exist. Set a 60-day plan to gather case studies, get consent or commission a simple evaluation before you scale the story.

Red: Change the Claim

One or more claims are overclaims or lack any evidence. Rewrite the claim or remove it. A smaller, honest story outperforms a big, fragile one every time.
