KPI Baseline Tracker

Workbook Contents

Baseline Metrics: Record your Day 0 numbers before any activity.

Weekly Tracker: 8‑week self‑paced log. Start as soon as you finish Module 1.

Dashboard: Auto‑updating summary with simple trend indicators from the Weekly Tracker.

KPI Menu: Menu of suggested KPIs by organisation type to help you choose what to track first.
Important Notes

• Complete your Baseline Metrics before you start any new movement‑building activity.
• Use the KPI Menu to confirm you are tracking the right metrics for your organisation type and stage.
• Choose 3 KPIs to focus on in your first 30 days, then add more from Week 5 onwards if you have capacity.
• The Weekly Tracker is an 8‑week log. Update it at least once a week while you work through the course and run your 30‑day campaign. It is fine if your 8 weeks run past the end of the course – the point is to see before and after movement, not to be perfect.
• The Dashboard reads everything from your Weekly Tracker automatically. Do not type into the Dashboard sheet directly.
